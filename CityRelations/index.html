City Relations

A game where you guess the relative location of two cities.

You will be given two cities and a direction and you must pick the one that is the furthest in that direction. For example, if told to choose the furthest south between Paris, Île-de-France, France and Austin, Texas, United States, you would pick Austin.

City data is taken from simplemaps.com's World Cities Database and US Cities Database.
